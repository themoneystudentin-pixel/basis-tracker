Annualised basis

How to read this

Annualised basis = (Futures − Spot) ÷ Spot × 365 ÷ days to expiry. It turns the futures premium into a yearly interest rate so every day is comparable.

Normal band = average ± 1.5 standard deviations of the last 20 entries for that stock (needs 10+ entries). Readings outside it are what matter.

  • Above band: futures buyers paying extra — leveraged longs crowding in. Bullish but fragile.
  • Below band / negative: futures sellers pushing price down — heavy shorts. First check for an upcoming ex-dividend date, which legitimately creates a discount.

Price + OI (same contract only):

  • Price ↑ OI ↑ — Long buildup
  • Price ↓ OI ↑ — Short buildup
  • Price ↑ OI ↓ — Short covering
  • Price ↓ OI ↓ — Long unwinding

When you switch to a new expiry, OI change is not comparable for that day and is shown as "Rollover".
